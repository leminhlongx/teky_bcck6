PUZZLE-GAMES
Puzzle games are a genre of video games that focus on problem-solving, logic, and critical thinking rather than action or storytelling. Players are challenged to complete tasks such as matching patterns, arranging objects, or solving riddles to progress. These games often test skills like memory, spatial reasoning, and strategy, rewarding creativity and persistence. Puzzle games can range from simple, casual experiences like matching tiles to complex titles requiring deep planning. Their main appeal lies in offering mental challenges, satisfying solutions, and engaging gameplay for players of all ages.
