ADVENTURE GAMES
Adventure games are video games that emphasize storytelling, exploration, and problem-solving over fast-paced action. Players usually guide a main character, interact with the environment, and solve puzzles to progress through the story. Classic versions include text-based and point-and-click games, while modern ones may use 3D worlds and cinematic styles. Key features often involve dialogue choices, object interaction, and critical thinking. The main appeal of adventure games lies in their immersive narratives, where player decisions and discoveries shape the experience. They attract players who prefer story-driven gameplay and intellectual challenges rather than combat.
