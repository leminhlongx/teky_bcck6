ROLE-PLAYING GAME
A role-playing game (RPG) is an interactive adventure where players take on the roles of unique characters in afictional world. Players develop their characters through choices, skills, and quests, often guided by a storyline filled with challenges and exploration. RPGs can be set in fantasy, sci-fi, or historical settings, offering immersive worlds with rich lore. Gameplay usually involves completing missions, battling enemies, solving puzzles, and making decisions that affect the outcome of the story. With character progression, customization, and strategic combat, RPGs combine storytelling and gameplay, allowing players to live out epic journeys and shape their own destinies.
